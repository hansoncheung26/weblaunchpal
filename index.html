FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

I already have a Google Business profile — do I still need a website?
Yes. Your Google profile gets people interested. Your website is where they decide to call you or move on. Without one, you're sending potential customers straight to your competitors.
What if I don't like how it looks?
We revise until it feels right. The demo is free and there's no commitment until you're happy with the direction. Most clients are surprised how close the first version is.
Is the pricing negotiable?
Always. Every business is different and we'd rather find something that works than lose a good fit over price. Just ask.
How do I know you'll actually deliver?
The demo comes first — before any payment, before any commitment. You see the actual website built for your business before deciding anything. If you don't like it, you walk away and pay nothing.
Do I pay anything upfront?
No. The demo is completely free. Payment only happens once you've seen the site, approved the direction, and decided to move forward.
Who am I actually working with?
Just me — Hanson, the founder. Not an agency, not an overseas team. You have my direct contact from day one and I handle everything personally.
What do you need from me to get started?
Almost nothing. Your business name, what you do, and any photos or branding you already have. We handle the rest and ask questions as we go.
What happens after the site goes live?
You get a full walkthrough of everything on your site. On a care plan we handle all updates going forward. Either way, the site is yours and we're always available if you need anything.
