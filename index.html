Questions people ask first

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. The demo is free. You only pay once you've seen the site, approved the direction, and decided to go ahead. If you don't like it, you walk away and owe nothing.

I already have a Google Business Profile. Do I still need a website?

Yes. Your Google profile gets people interested, and your website is where they decide whether to call you. Without one, a lot of those people move on to a competitor who has one.

Can you redesign the website I already have?

Yes. Send me your current address in the form. I'll keep what works, fix what doesn't, and show you the new version as a demo before anything changes on your live site.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your business name, what you do, and any photos or branding you already have. I handle the rest and ask questions as I go.

Is the pricing negotiable?

Yes. Every business is different, and I'd rather find a price that works than lose a good fit over it. Just ask.

What happens after the site goes live?

You get a walkthrough of everything, and the site is yours. On a care plan I handle updates for you. Either way, you can reach me whenever you need something.
